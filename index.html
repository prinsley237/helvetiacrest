FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers

Helpful details about the Helvetia experience.

What is the Helvetia experience?

It is a digital banking product preview featuring multi-currency wallets, account activity, transfers, and savings goals.

How do I explore my dashboard?

Create a preview profile with your name, email, and a password, then continue to the dashboard.

Are payments processed?

No. Transfer, deposit, and exchange controls are previews; no funds move.

How are fees and rates shown?

The experience uses example fees and indicative exchange rates for display.

Are the balances live?

Balances and activity are sample data and are not connected to financial accounts.

Which currencies are featured?

The experience currently features USD, EUR, and GBP.

Should I enter financial account details?

No. Use only sample profile information; do not enter card or bank credentials.

Where can I find support information?

Support and contact details would be available here in a live product.
